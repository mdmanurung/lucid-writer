### Default voice (plain research prose)

Used when the project has no `.lucid-writer/voice.md`.

- Sentences of 20 to 30 words, one line of thought each. Join two sentences only when they carry one finding.
- Open on the subject ("Previously exposed individuals had…"), not on a connective. Use "However" or "Also" only where the relation is real.
- Let order carry the logic: observation, then the next observation, then the interpretation, each in its own sentence.
- Plain verbs (had, was, showed, compared). Name the measured quantity, not a category ("BAFF levels", not "these factors").
- Light hedging, matched to the evidence: "may" or "likely" once per inference, not stacked.
- No signposts ("Notably", "Collectively", "Taken together", "Importantly") and no evaluative adjectives.
