Higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency was associated with prolonged parasite-free survival in 212 adults (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55–0.95; p = 0.018), persisting after adjustment for age and prior malaria episodes (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.57–0.99). Top tertile participants remained parasite-free for a median of 41 days compared with 27 days in the bottom tertile. These cells may contribute to early parasite control, though causality cannot be determined from this observational study.
