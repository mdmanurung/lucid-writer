In 150 adults with type 2 diabetes, higher fasting plasma lactate was associated with higher HbA1c (rho = 0.31, 95% CI 0.15–0.45) and remained so after adjustment for age and BMI (rho = 0.28, 95% CI 0.11–0.43). Cross-sectional design prevents determining causality between lactate and glycaemic control or the role of confounding.
