In 150 adults with type 2 diabetes, higher fasting plasma lactate was associated with higher HbA1c (rho = 0.31, 95% CI 0.15–0.45), similarly after adjustment for age and BMI (adjusted rho = 0.28, 95% CI 0.11–0.43). Because the data are cross-sectional, they cannot establish the direction of this association or exclude confounding.
