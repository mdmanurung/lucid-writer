Among 212 adults, higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency associated with longer parasitaemia-free time (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55–0.95; p = 0.018), with persistence after adjustment for age and prior malaria (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.57–0.99). Participants in the top tertile remained parasite-free for 41 days versus 27 days in the bottom tertile. Whether these cells are directly protective remains unclear in this observational study.
