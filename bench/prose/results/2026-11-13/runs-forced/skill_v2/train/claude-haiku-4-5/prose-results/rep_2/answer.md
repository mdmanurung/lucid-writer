In 212 adults, higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency predicted longer parasitaemia-free periods (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55–0.95; p = 0.018) and persisted after adjustment for age and prior malaria episodes (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.57–0.99). Participants in the top tertile remained parasite-free 41 days versus 27 days in the bottom tertile. These cells may thus contribute to early parasite control, though causality cannot be determined from this observational design.
