In 150 adults with type 2 diabetes, higher fasting plasma lactate was associated with higher HbA1c (rho = 0.31, 95% CI 0.15–0.45), remaining associated after adjustment for age and BMI (adjusted rho = 0.28, 95% CI 0.11–0.43). The cross-sectional design does not establish the direction of association or identify confounders.
