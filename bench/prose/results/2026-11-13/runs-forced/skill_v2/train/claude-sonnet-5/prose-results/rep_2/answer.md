In this cohort of 212 adults, higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency was associated with longer time to parasitaemia (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55–0.95; p = 0.018), persisting after adjustment for age and prior malaria episodes (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.57–0.99). Median parasite-free time was 41 days in the top tertile versus 27 in the bottom. These cells may thus aid early parasite control, though the observational design cannot establish protection.
