In 150 adults with type 2 diabetes, higher fasting plasma lactate correlated with higher HbA1c (rho = 0.31, 95% CI 0.15–0.45), similarly after adjustment for age and BMI (adjusted rho = 0.28, 95% CI 0.11–0.43). As the data are cross-sectional, they cannot establish causal direction or rule out confounding.
