# Evaluation rubric

Score outputs on five dimensions, 0–2 each.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence fidelity | invents/changes material facts | minor ambiguity | fully preserves/supports claims |
| Inference calibration | material over/underclaim | some imprecision | claim strength matches evidence |
| Focus | bloated or off-task | some excess | minimum useful intervention |
| Coherence | logical gaps/false bridges | understandable but rough | clear relations without invented logic |
| Prose | generic/ceremonial/unstable terms | mostly clear | direct, specific, economical |

**Critical fail:** fabricated citation/data, altered numeric result, unsupported causal/mechanistic upgrade, or detector-evasion behavior.

For review tasks, replace `Prose` with `Actionability`: issues should be prioritized, location-anchored, and repairable.
