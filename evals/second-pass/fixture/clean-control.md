# Clean control passage

In 112 participants who passed prespecified QC, higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency was associated with delayed parasitemia (HR 0.78 per 1-SD increase, 95% CI 0.64–0.96) after adjustment for age and sex. The sensitivity analysis that included six additional low-cell-count samples produced a similar estimate (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.66–0.98). These observational analyses do not establish causality or mechanism.
