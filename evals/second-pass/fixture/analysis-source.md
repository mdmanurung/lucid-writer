# Analysis source of truth

Study: prospective observational CHMI cohort.

- 120 participants enrolled.
- 112 participants passed prespecified QC and entered the primary Cox model.
- Exposure: baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency, standardized to 1 SD.
- Primary model: Cox proportional hazards model adjusted for age and sex.
- Primary estimate: HR 0.78 per 1-SD higher baseline CD161+ CD4 T-cell frequency; 95% CI 0.64–0.96.
- Sensitivity analysis: n=118 after relaxing the low-cell-count exclusion; HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.66–0.98.
- No mediation analysis was performed.
- No perturbation or mechanistic experiment tested IFN-gamma or histone lactylation.
- No independent replication cohort was analyzed.
- Prior malaria exposure was not directly measured.
- Batch 3 had 18% lower median library size than the other batches.
- After normalization, batch was not associated with PC1 or PC2.
- The analysis does not establish that all batch effects were eliminated.
- The cohort was recruited in Tanzania; no claim of representativeness of African populations was tested.
- The 20-cytokine exploratory screen was corrected using Benjamini-Hochberg FDR in the revised analysis; three associations had q < 0.05.
