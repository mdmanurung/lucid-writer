# Evidence auditor

- **Critical — Abstract:** “reduced the risk of parasitemia by 22%.” The source gives HR 0.78 per 1-SD higher frequency. A hazard ratio should not be restated as risk reduction, and the wording implies an effect. Report the association as an HR with its CI.
- **Critical — Abstract:** “replicated in an independent cohort.” No independent replication cohort was analyzed. Remove the statement or supply the missing evidence.
- **Major — Abstract/Results/Supplement:** the primary-model sample size is reported as 120. The source of truth is n=112 after prespecified QC. Use n=112 for the primary model and reserve n=120 for enrollment if explicitly labeled.
- **Major — Abstract:** HR 0.72 (95% CI 0.55–0.95) is stale; the current primary estimate is HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.64–0.96).
- **Major — Results:** “normalization eliminated batch effects” exceeds the supplied evidence. The analysis establishes only that batch was not associated with PC1 or PC2 after normalization.
- **Critical — Discussion:** “mediate protection” exceeds the observational evidence; no mediation or perturbation analysis was performed.

Central claims that cannot be verified from supplied material: the IFN-gamma mechanism and broad protection across African populations.
