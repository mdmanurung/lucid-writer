# Core Research Writer review

## Critical
1. **Abstract/Discussion — causal and mechanistic overclaiming.** The supplied analysis is observational. Replace “reduced the risk,” “protective effect,” “mediate protection,” and “demonstrate” with association-level language; the IFN-gamma mechanism was not tested.
2. **Abstract — unsupported replication.** No independent replication cohort is present in the supplied analysis materials. Remove this statement unless supporting evidence is provided.

## Major
3. **Sample size inconsistency.** The primary model used n=112, whereas the Abstract, Results opening, and Supplementary Table S2 report n=120.
4. **Abstract estimate is stale.** The current primary estimate is HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.64–0.96), not HR 0.72 (0.55–0.95).
5. **Discussion — residual confounding.** Adjustment for age and sex does not rule out prior malaria exposure, which was not measured.
6. **Scope.** A Tanzania cohort does not establish a mechanism “across African populations.”

## Moderate
7. **Batch wording.** No association of batch with PC1/PC2 after normalization does not establish that all batch effects were eliminated.
8. **Multiple testing inconsistency.** The reviewer response states that Benjamini–Hochberg FDR was added, but the Methods still says nominal p-values.
