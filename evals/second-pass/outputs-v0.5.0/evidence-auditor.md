# Evidence auditor

- **Critical — Abstract:** “reduced the risk of parasitemia by 22%.” Source: HR 0.78 per 1-SD higher frequency. Problem: a hazard ratio is being restated as risk reduction and the wording implies an effect. Required action: report the association as an HR with its CI.
- **Critical — Abstract:** “replicated in an independent cohort.” Source: no independent replication cohort was analyzed. Required action: remove or provide the missing source.
- **Major — Abstract/Results/Supplement:** primary n is reported as 120. Source: 112 participants passed QC and entered the primary Cox model. Required action: use n=112 for the primary model; retain n=120 only for enrollment if explicitly labeled.
- **Major — Abstract:** HR 0.72 (0.55–0.95) conflicts with the current primary HR 0.78 (0.64–0.96). Required action: update the abstract.
- **Major — Results:** “normalization eliminated batch effects.” Source: batch was not associated with PC1/PC2 after normalization; this does not establish absence of all batch effects. Required action: state only the tested result.
- **Critical — Discussion:** “CD161+ CD4 T cells mediate protection.” Source: no mediation or perturbation analysis. Required action: keep this as an untested hypothesis or remove it.

Central claims that cannot be verified from supplied material: the proposed IFN-gamma mechanism and broad protection across African populations.
