# Notices and acknowledgements

Lucid Writer is an original synthesis. The repository documents the sources and public implementations that informed its design in `docs/methodology.md`.

## Coherent Writing

The coherence workflow was informed by:

- `ericmjl/skills`, `skills/coherent-writing`
- https://github.com/ericmjl/skills/tree/main/skills/coherent-writing
- licensed under the MIT License in the upstream repository.

Lucid Writer re-expresses and adapts the workflow for evidence-bounded research prose rather than copying the upstream skill wholesale.

The guard split, citation-verdict scale, figure–text flags, "not checked" list, and reviewer lenses were informed by:

- `seandavi/scriptorium`, © 2026 Sean Davis
- https://github.com/seandavi/scriptorium
- code licensed under MIT; documentation and knowledge notes under CC BY 4.0.

## Humanizer

The pattern list in `skills/lucid-writer/references/patterns.md` and the matching `lint.py` rules were informed by:

- `blader/humanizer`, © 2025 Siqi Chen
